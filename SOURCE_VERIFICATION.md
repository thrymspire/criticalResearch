This ledger records what was actually checked against the nine primary/source documents supplied for the public research artifact. The PDFs themselves are **not** included in the public GitHub Pages package; they remain source evidence, not redistributed site assets.

## Publication and privacy boundary

- No source PDF is bundled or redistributed in the GitHub Pages site.
- No walking/mobility telemetry, trajectory data, Wi-Fi positioning matrix, GPS/geolocation dataset, or derived movement/OD matrix is included.
- Public static delivery cannot prevent copying of visible HTML/SVG/audio. Protection is implemented through provenance marking, rights notices, controlled source publication, and export metadata rather than fake DRM.
