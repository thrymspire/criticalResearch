## Executive Overview

**Critical Research** unifies a diverse 143-paper research corpus spanning 16 academic disciplines—ranging from analytical philosophy and structural equation modeling to psychophysiology, narratology, and autonomous cognitive state machines—into a singular, cohesive computational workstation.

Rather than relying on fragmented PDF summaries or third-party web frameworks, the entire synthesis is embodied as a **monolithic, self-contained HTML5/Canvas/SVG artifact** (`index.html`). The project operates under strict zero-dependency engineering constraints: no external fonts, no CDN scripts, no heavy raster bloat, and no remote runtime dependencies.
